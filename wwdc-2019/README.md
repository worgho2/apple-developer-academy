# wwdc-2019

Spitro, my Swift Playgrounds submission for the WWDC 2019 scholarship at the Apple Developer Academy. Spitros are text-based SpriteKit creatures that orbit, spin and change color, and the playground lets you tweak their creation parameters and watch how the collective behaves.

- `spitro.playgroundbook` is the submitted playground book, with three pages that introduce the elements, let you customize them, and end in an interactive scene. `spitro.playgroundbook.rar` and `spitro_OLD.playgroundbook.zip` are archived copies.
- `primeiro.playground`, `segundo.playground` and `terceiro.playground` are the exploratory steps that led to the book: a 60-second visual intro, the parameter playground, and the collective behaviour test.
- `liveview.playground` is a separate "The Deep Dive" ocean live view experiment using Metal Performance Shaders.

## Run

Open the `.playgroundbook` in Swift Playgrounds on iPad, or any `.playground` in Xcode.
